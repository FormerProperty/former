How it works

  1. i.

    Castles, manors, country houses and estates are gathered from Zoopla each week, across England, Scotland and Wales.

  2. ii.

    Each is read and judged: a castle or tower house; a listed house that is unmistakably grand; or a manor or country house with real land. Flats in divided mansions, new builds and houses that are merely large are left out. Buildings that began as something else belong to our sister site, formerly.

  3. iii.

    What passes appears here, in the weekly email and on social media, with a link to the full listing on Zoopla.

  4. iv.

    Every house is checked again about once a month. When a listing disappears from Zoopla, it comes off here. When it goes under offer, it carries an Under offer flag for up to a month, then comes off unless it returns to the market. A sale can take a few weeks to show here, so always check the listing itself.

Sources

Zoopla
Every listing here was found on Zoopla, and every link goes back to it. Prices, bedrooms and locations come from the listing; the descriptions are our own words. Agents' photographs and text are not copied.
Wikimedia Commons
Many photographs come from Wikimedia Commons, including entries to Wiki Loves Monuments. Each is credited to its photographer with its licence, and links to the original.
Geograph
Other photographs are from the Geograph project, which aims to photograph every square kilometre of Britain. Each is credited to its photographer and used under its Creative Commons licence.
Ordnance Survey
The labels on each picture borrow the abbreviations printed on old Ordnance Survey maps. No Ordnance Survey data is used.
