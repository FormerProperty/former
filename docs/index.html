How it works

  • Found

    New listings that mention a former use are collected from England's property portals every week.

  • Checked

    Each one is tested: was this building, or a whole part of it, built for something other than living in?

  • Published

    What passes appears here, in the weekly email and on social media, with a link to the original listing.
