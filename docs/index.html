How it works

  1. i.

    Listings that mention a former use are gathered from Zoopla each week.

  2. ii.

    Each is judged against one test: was this building, or a substantial part of it, built for something other than living in? Barn conversions, flats in larger developments and houses that merely stand on Chapel Lane are left out. Most of the sorting is done by software, so the odd mistake will slip through. If you spot one, press Report on the listing and we will take another look.

  3. iii.

    What passes appears here, in the weekly email and on social media, with a link to the full listing on Zoopla.

Sources

Zoopla
Every listing here was found on Zoopla, and every link goes back to it. Prices, bedrooms and locations come from the listing; the descriptions are our own words. Agents' photographs and text are not copied.
Geograph
Photographs are from the Geograph project, which aims to photograph every square kilometre of Britain. Each is credited to its photographer and used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 licence. A photo may show a building before or during its conversion.
Ordnance Survey
The labels on each picture borrow the abbreviations printed on old Ordnance Survey maps. No Ordnance Survey data is used.
