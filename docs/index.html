How it works

  1. i.

    New listings that mention a former use are gathered from England's property portals each week.

  2. ii.

    Each is checked by hand: was this building, or a substantial part of it, built for something other than living in? Barn conversions, flats in larger developments and houses that merely stand on Chapel Lane are left out.

  3. iii.

    What passes appears here, in the weekly email and on social media, with a link to the agent's own listing.
