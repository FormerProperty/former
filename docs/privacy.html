formerly. privacy

formerly. is a free listing of buildings for sale in England that were originally built as something other than homes. It is run by [your name] as a not-for-profit project. This page explains what personal information we hold and what we do with it.

What we collect

The website itself does not collect personal information, use cookies or track visitors. We only hold personal information if you sign up for the weekly email. Then we hold:

  • your email address
  • the areas or building types you choose to hear about, if you choose any
  • basic delivery records, such as whether an email was delivered or opened

Why we hold it

We hold your email address only to send you the formerly. weekly email you asked for. Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give when you subscribe and confirm your address.

Who processes it

Our mailing list is run by [Buttondown, Inc.], which stores your details and sends the emails on our behalf. Their privacy policy is at [link]. Your details may be stored outside the UK, under safeguards approved for international transfers.

We never sell, share or rent your details, and we never use them for anything else.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe. When you do, we delete them, except for a record that the address has unsubscribed, which stops it being added again by mistake.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. You also have the right to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent. To do any of these, email [contact address].

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Listings

Each building on formerly. links to its original advert. We are not an estate agent and we do not handle enquiries or sales. Any information you give an agent is held by that agent under their own privacy policy.
